# BIZZARO BANGALORE
## Case File #02: THE MANAGER

---

### BEAT 1 — COLD OPEN: THE LEAVE REQUEST

*Visual:*
Paper-cutout employee at a desk late at night, cursor hovering over "Send" on a leave request form — stepped stop-motion hesitation, same anxious-jitter language as Episode 1's hand-raise gesture. A beat of held tension — clock on the wall ticking, paper-cutout jitter.

*On-screen text (kinetic, ransom-note style, notification popup):*
`LEAVE REQUEST — SUBMITTED`

*Beat:*
Almost instantly, a reply notification pops in — no dramatic wait.

*Manager's reply (on-screen, notification-style text):*
`Approved. Don't check Teams while you're gone.`

*VO (begins right as the notification lands):*
"Every day, thousands of leave requests are submitted in Bangalore. This is the story of the one that got approved without a follow-up call."

*Sound:*
Late-night quiet — a single ambient hum (fan/AC), the notification "ping" landing sharply against the silence. No dialogue needed.

---

### BEAT 2 — THE STAT CARD

*Visual:*
Hard cut to full-frame mock-infographic, torn newsprint strips forming a bar chart, numbers stamped in like evidence tags — same visual language as Episode 1's stat card.

*VO:*
"According to our survey, 89% of leave requests in Bangalore go unanswered for at least 48 hours. Of the 11% that get approved — 94% come with a passive-aggressive follow-up call anyway."

*On-screen text (kinetic, stat-card style):*
`89% OF LEAVE REQUESTS GO UNANSWERED FOR 48+ HOURS`
`OF THOSE APPROVED: 94% GET A FOLLOW-UP CALL ANYWAY`

*Cliffhanger beat — final line, held on a freeze of the stat card:*
"Which means, statistically... this approval should never have happened — let alone in under a minute."

Hard cut to black. Silence. (Leads into tear-transition / testimony beat.)

---

### BEAT 3 — TRANSITION: TEAR REVEAL

*Visual:*
The frozen stat-card graphic tears away — same jagged rip technique as Episode 1 — revealing photoreal footage underneath. Tear widens frame by frame until fully peeled, settling into the full webcam frame.

*Sound:*
The sharper paper-rip SFX (distinct from the softer page-flip riffle), signaling the style shift.

*Optional stamp beat:*
`FOOTAGE: WITNESS TESTIMONY` flashes briefly before settling into the lower-third.

---

### BEAT 4 — WITNESS TESTIMONY (photoreal)

*Visual:*
Mock video-call frame, employee seated at home, low-bitrate webcam texture. Lower-third:
`WITNESS — NAME WITHHELD`
`SURVIVOR, INCIDENT #0002`

*Correspondent (off-screen):* "Can you walk us through what happened?"

*Employee (deadpan-traumatized, building through the list):*
"I sent the request at 11:47 PM. Expecting silence. Maybe a reply Monday, if I was lucky. He replied in under a minute. No questions. No 'let's discuss in standup.' Just... approved."

*Visual beat during the list:*
Each detail gets a tiny on-screen tick/checkmark stamp — evidence-checklist style, newsprint texture, small stamp-sound punctuation per beat:
✓ `REPLIED IN UNDER 1 MINUTE`
✓ `NO FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS`
✓ `NO "LET'S DISCUSS" DEFLECTION`

*Beat — slight pause, eyes drift, replaying the memory:*
"He even said don't check Teams. I've never not checked Teams on leave. I didn't know what to do with myself."

*Visual beat:*
One final tick stamps in, slightly delayed from the others for comic timing:
✓ `TOLD ME TO LOG OFF`

*Sound:*
Muffled room tone, small audio-dropout glitch on "I didn't know what to do with myself" for comic emphasis.

---

### BEAT 5 — EXPERT COMMENTARY (photoreal)

*Visual:*
Straight cut to Dr. Nagesh Ramamurthy's studio setup, same lower-third style as Episode 1:
`DR. NAGESH RAMAMURTHY, ORGANIZATIONAL BEHAVIOURIST`
`BANGALORE INSTITUTE OF TRANSIT STUDIES*`
*(*institute unaccredited)*

*Dr. Ramamurthy (mid-sentence, total academic confidence, completely wrong):*
"—this is a classic case of S.T.F.U. Syndrome. Suddenly Transparent, Fair & Understanding. Under prolonged exposure to healthy work-life boundaries, a manager's brain can spontaneously begin... communicating clearly. It's rare. It's poorly understood. Some don't recover.

We've seen early symptoms before — a manager saying 'good point' in a meeting without adding a 'but.' Giving credit without being asked twice. These are usually contained. This... this progressed."

*Beat:*
"In advanced cases, like this one, the subject may even apologize first — before being asked to."

*Beat — leans in, delivers the kicker with total sincerity:*
"If anything, he's the one who needs the leave now."

*Visual beat — zoom-in treatment:*
As he delivers the kicker, camera push-zooms into his face. Right as he finishes, hard cut/whip to the mock whiteboard behind him, filling the frame. Kicker line slams on as giant vertical-split kinetic type, stamped in line by line with a 2–3 frame stagger:
`IF ANYTHING`
`HE'S THE ONE`
`WHO NEEDS`
`THE LEAVE`
`NOW`
Whiteboard doodles (arrows, laptop-closing sketch) remain faintly visible behind the text, slightly blurred from the zoom.

*Sound:*
Stamp-thud on each line landing, building to "NOW" hitting hardest.

---

### BEAT 6 — INSTITUTIONAL FALLOUT

*Transition:* Reverse tear-reveal back into paper-cutout world.

*Visual:*
Newspaper-collage headline slam, kinetic type:
`MANAGERS' ASSOCIATION WRITES TO HR`
`"COMPENSATE THOSE SUFFERING FROM S.T.F.U. SYNDROME"`

*VO (deadpan, escalating bureaucratic absurdity):*
"The Bangalore Managers' Association has formally petitioned HR leadership, demanding compensation for members affected by S.T.F.U. Syndrome."

*Sound:*
Newspaper-rustle texture, stamp-thud as headline lands.

---

*Transition:* Tear-reveal to photoreal — cut to the manager himself (first time shown on camera), same webcam-testimony setup as the employee's earlier interview. Lower-third:
`WITNESS — MANAGER, NAME WITHHELD`
`NOW SEEKING COMPENSATION`

*Manager (flat, aggrieved, sincere):*
"This week alone, I said 'no worries' four times. Genuinely. No worries. I don't even know who I am anymore."

*Sound:*
Muffled room tone, matching testimony register.

---

*Cut back to paper-cutout newspaper collage* — second headline slams in, his own quote recontextualized:
`MANAGER FILES OWN CLAIM`
`"I DON'T EVEN KNOW WHO I AM ANYMORE"`

---

### BEAT 7 — CORRESPONDENT PSA (photoreal)

*Visual:*
Cut to the correspondent, on camera for the first time, same news-anchor-sign-off framing as Episode 1 — direct address, deadpan.

*Correspondent:*
"If your manager has approved a request without a follow-up call — or said 'no worries' and meant it — do not panic. This is likely a mild case. Monitor for further symptoms."

*Beat — slight pause, false gravity:*
"You are not alone."

*Sound:*
Same polished studio room-tone as the expert interview.

---

### BEAT 8 — THE COMMITTEE (paper-cutout, quick beat)

*Transition:* Tear-reveal back into paper-cutout world.

*Visual:*
One final headline slam:
`HR FORMS COMMITTEE TO STUDY S.T.F.U. SYNDROME`
`MEETING RESCHEDULED DUE TO OFFICE RANGOLI COMPETITION`

*VO (dry, quick):*
"HR has since formed a committee to study the matter further."

*Sound:*
Stamp-thud, quick paper-rustle.

---

### BEAT 9 — FULL-CIRCLE CLOSE

*Visual:*
Cut back to the manager, paper-cutout, at his desk late at night — same framing as Beat 1's leave-request scene, now from his side. He glances at his phone, another late-night message coming in... and closes his laptop instead of replying.

*VO (final line, quieter, more ominous than deadpan):*
"As for the manager himself — still at his desk most nights. Still responding fast. Boundaries... intact. For now."

*Visual beat:*
Freeze frame on him closing the laptop. Slow desaturate to grayscale, halftone texture creeping in.

*Final stamp:*
`CASE FILE #0002 — CLOSED` — thuds onto screen, hard cut to black.
