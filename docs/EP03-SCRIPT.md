# BIZZARO BANGALORE
## Case File #03: THE LANDLORD

---

### SHOT 1 — COLD OPEN: THE DEPOSIT (0:00–0:12)

*Visual:*
One continuous camera-drag shot, paper-cutout style, stepped stop-motion movement. Camera pans slowly across a bare wall — catching a visible crack, then a faded poster-patch. Drag continues onto a stain cutout (water-mark/blotch, torn edges). Camera tilts down to a cracked floor tile. Drag settles on the tenant, standing amid all this "damage," holding his keys, visibly bracing.

*Tenant (nervous, pre-emptive):*
"About the deposit, sir — the wall, that stain, that was already like that when I—"

*Landlord enters frame, cutting him off — already holding a stack of cutout currency notes. No inspection, no glance at the walls/stain/floor.*

*Landlord (flat, already handing it over):*
"Forget it. Full amount. Here."

*Visual beat:* Tenant's hands instinctively reach out to take it, still mid-flinch, confused rather than relieved.

*VO (begins right as the cash changes hands):*
"Every day, thousands of security deposits are 'settled' in Bangalore. This is the story of the one that was returned in full."

*Sound:*
Low, dry room-echo builds through the wall/stain/floor drag (empty-flat reverb). Soft paper-drag rustle on each camera move. Cash-counting/stack rustle as the notes transfer.

---

### SHOT 2 — THE STAT CARD (0:12–0:20)

*Visual:*
Hard cut to full-frame mock-infographic, newspaper-collage bar chart — torn newsprint strips, numbers stamped in like evidence tags.

*VO:*
"According to our survey, 98% of security deposits in Bangalore are returned with a standard one-month deduction. Of the remaining 2% — 85% take over six months to actually hand it over."

*On-screen text (kinetic, stat-card style):*
`98% OF DEPOSITS RETURNED WITH A STANDARD ONE-MONTH DEDUCTION`
`OF THE REMAINING 2%: 85% TAKE OVER 6 MONTHS TO PAY`

*Cliffhanger beat — final line, held on a freeze of the stat card:*
"Which means, statistically... this landlord shouldn't exist."

Hard cut to black. Silence. (Leads into tear-transition / testimony beat.)

---

### SHOT 3 — TRANSITION: TEAR REVEAL

*Visual:*
The frozen stat-card graphic tears away — same jagged rip technique as prior episodes — revealing photoreal footage underneath. Tear widens frame by frame until fully peeled, settling into the full webcam frame.

*Sound:*
Sharp paper-rip SFX (distinct from the softer page-flip riffle), signaling the style shift.

*Optional stamp beat:*
`FOOTAGE: WITNESS TESTIMONY` flashes briefly before settling into the lower-third.

---

### SHOT 4 — WITNESS TESTIMONY (photoreal)

*Visual:*
Mock video-call frame, tenant seated at home, low-bitrate webcam texture. Lower-third:
`WITNESS — NAME WITHHELD`
`SURVIVOR, INCIDENT #0003`

*Tenant (deadpan-traumatized, building through the list):*
"I had my whole defense ready. I'd rehearsed it in the mirror. 'That stain was there before I moved in.' No inspection. No deduction. No lecture. He just handed it over. Full amount."

*Visual beat during the list:*
Each item gets a tiny tick/checkmark stamp as he says it — evidence-checklist style, newsprint texture, small stamp-sound punctuation per beat:
✓ `NO INSPECTION`
✓ `NO DEDUCTION`
✓ `NO LECTURE`

*Beat — slight pause, eyes drift, replaying the memory:*
"I still have the speech saved in my notes app. I don't know what to do with it now."

*Sound:*
Muffled room tone, small audio-dropout glitch on "I don't know what to do with it now" for comic emphasis.

---

### SHOT 5 — EXPERT COMMENTARY (photoreal)

*Visual:*
Straight cut to Dr. Nagesh Ramamurthy's studio setup, same lower-third style as prior episodes:
`DR. NAGESH RAMAMURTHY, RESIDENTIAL BEHAVIOURIST`
`BANGALORE INSTITUTE OF TRANSIT STUDIES*`
*(*institute unaccredited)*

*Dr. Ramamurthy (mid-sentence, total academic confidence, completely wrong):*
"—this is a textbook case of F.A.Q. Syndrome. Full Amount, Questions-free. What we're seeing is a rare psychological reflex: when a tenant causes zero disturbances for the full lease term, some landlords experience a kind of involuntary... gratitude response. It's been observed maybe four, five times in recorded history.

Early signs are subtle — a landlord not raising rent by 20% at renewal, replying to a maintenance request within the week. Left unchecked, it can progress to this: full deposit, same day, no fuss."

*Beat — leans in, delivers the kicker with total sincerity:*
"Frankly, we're lucky he didn't offer to repaint it himself."

*Visual beat:*
Mock whiteboard behind him — arrows connecting "ON-TIME RENT," "NO 2AM CALLS," and a doodle of a paintbrush, none of it logically connected.

---

### SHOT 6 — INSTITUTIONAL FALLOUT

*Transition:* Reverse tear-reveal back into paper-cutout world.

*Visual:*
Newspaper-collage headline slam, kinetic type:
`LANDLORDS' ASSOCIATION WRITES TO GOVERNMENT`
`"COMPENSATE THOSE SUFFERING FROM F.A.Q. SYNDROME"`

*VO (deadpan, escalating bureaucratic absurdity):*
"The Bangalore Landlords' Association has formally petitioned the housing ministry, demanding compensation for members affected by F.A.Q. Syndrome."

*Sound:*
Newspaper-rustle texture, stamp-thud as headline lands.

---

*Transition:* Tear-reveal to photoreal — cut back to the tenant (from Shot 4), same webcam setup, now more visibly shaken. Lower-third:
`WITNESS — NAME WITHHELD`
`NOW SEEKING COMPENSATION`

*Tenant (flat, aggrieved, sincere):*
"He gets compensation for being nice? I had a whole speech prepared. Rehearsed. Ready. I never got to use it. Do you know what that does to a person?"

*Sound:*
Muffled room tone, matching earlier testimony register.

---

*Cut back to paper-cutout newspaper collage* — second headline slams in, his own quote recontextualized:
`VICTIM FILES OWN CLAIM`
`"I NEVER GOT TO USE MY SPEECH"`

---

*Correspondent:*
"If your landlord has returned your deposit in full — without inspection, without deduction — do not panic. Keep your rehearsed speech somewhere safe. You may need it for the next one."

*Beat — slight pause, false gravity:*
"You are not alone."

*Sound:*
Same polished studio room-tone as the expert interview.

### SHOT 8 — THE COMMITTEE (paper-cutout, quick beat)

### SHOT 9 — FULL-CIRCLE CLOSE

*Visual:*
Cut back to the landlord, paper-cutout, standing in the now-empty flat — same framing as Shot 1's opening image. He glances at the cracked floor tile, the stain, the wall crack — beat of consideration — then simply pulls the door shut without inspecting further, cash already gone.

*VO (final line, quieter, more ominous than deadpan):*
"As for the landlord himself — already looking for the next tenant. Deposit policy... unchanged. For now."

*Visual beat:*
Freeze frame on the door closing. Slow desaturate to grayscale, halftone texture creeping in — mirroring Shot 1's opening treatment.

*Final stamp:*
`CASE FILE #0003 — CLOSED` — thuds onto screen, hard cut to black.
